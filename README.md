# 📊 Production Working Hours Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze employee productivity, working hours, overtime, earnings, workforce distribution, and operational costs.

The project uses employee workforce data prepared in Excel and transforms it into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to understand:

- How many hours employees were planned to work
- How many hours they actually worked
- Where working-hour shortfalls occurred
- How much overtime was performed
- How much overtime cost the organization
- Which cities generated the highest overtime earnings
- How workload is distributed across divisions
- Employee-wise earnings performance
- Gender diversity
- Average hourly rates across job roles
- Budget allocation versus employee earnings

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Workforce KPI Overview

Provides a high-level summary of:

- Total employee records
- Planned hours
- Worked hours
- Shortfall hours
- Overtime hours
- Earnings
- Overtime cost
- Allocated budget

### 2. Division-Wise Work Distribution

Compares planned hours with actual worked hours across:

- Photography
- Supporting
- Management

Photography represents the largest workload category in the dashboard.

### 3. City-Wise Overtime Earnings

Analyzes overtime spending across different cities, including:

- Toronto
- Tokyo
- Sydney
- Mumbai
- Mexican
- Chicago
- Cape Town
- Berlin
- Barcelona

Toronto and Tokyo show the highest overtime earnings at approximately ₹539K each.

### 4. Employee-Wise Earnings

Compares earnings across individual employees to identify high and low earning contributors.

### 5. Overtime Worked Hours

Provides employee-level visibility into overtime workload and helps identify employees with higher overtime exposure.

### 6. Gender Diversity

Shows the workforce distribution by gender:

- Male: 52.38%
- Female: 47.62%

### 7. Average Hourly Rate

Compares average hourly rates by job role:

- Cameraman: ₹86
- Coordinator: ₹78
- Assistant: ₹65

### 8. Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides filters for:

- City
- Division

This allows users to explore workforce performance at a more granular level.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Workforce Utilization

Total worked hours reached approximately 368.1K compared with 351.3K planned hours, indicating that actual workload exceeded planned capacity.

### Overtime

Employees accumulated approximately 21.22K overtime hours, resulting in approximately ₹2.75M in overtime costs.

### Earnings & Budget

Total employee earnings were approximately ₹30.1M against an allocated budget of ₹28.78M.

### Geographic Overtime

Toronto and Tokyo recorded the highest overtime earnings at approximately ₹539K each.

### Division Performance

Photography accounts for the largest share of working hours, making it the most significant workload division.

### Workforce Diversity

The workforce consists of approximately 52.38% male and 47.62% female employees.
